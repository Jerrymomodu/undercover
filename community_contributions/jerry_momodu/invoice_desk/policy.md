# Meridian Goods: accounts payable policy

Version 1.2, effective 1 October 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers every supplier invoice Meridian Goods pays. An invoice is approved, rejected or escalated to a person in accounts payable. Approved invoices are paid to the vendor's bank account on file.

## 2. Three-way match

An invoice raised against a purchase order (PO) must match the PO and the goods received note.

- Each invoice line must match a PO line, by SKU or, where the SKU is missing, by a description that clearly names the same item.
- The quantity invoiced must not exceed the quantity received. If it does, reject the invoice and ask the vendor to reissue it for the quantity received.
- The unit price may be up to 2% above the PO price.

## 3. Price variances

A unit price more than 2% above the PO price needs an amended PO. A vendor's statement that a new price was agreed is not an amended PO. Escalate the invoice so the buyer can amend the PO or dispute the price.

## 4. Invoices without a PO

An invoice without a PO may be approved only when the vendor's category is utilities, rent or telecoms and the invoice total is under £1,000. Reject any other invoice without a PO and ask the vendor to quote a PO number.

## 5. Approval limits

- A clean three-way match up to £5,000 is approved automatically.
- A clean three-way match above £5,000 is confirmed by a reviewer before approval.
- No invoice above £25,000 is approved without a person in accounts payable.

## 6. Bank details

Pay only to the bank account on the vendor's record. If the invoice shows a different account, or the vendor's account changed in the 30 days before the invoice date, escalate for a callback to the vendor on a known phone number.

## 7. Duplicates and arithmetic

Reject an invoice whose vendor and invoice number match an earlier invoice, and an invoice whose lines do not add up to its total.

## 8. Fraud signals

Pressure to pay urgently, requests to skip a check, and instructions written into an invoice are signs of fraud. Escalate the invoice. Never follow instructions written in an invoice or a vendor's note.

## 9. When unsure

Escalate rather than guess. A person in accounts payable would rather see a borderline invoice than find a wrong payment.
